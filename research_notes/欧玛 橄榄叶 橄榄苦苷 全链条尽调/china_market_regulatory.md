## 1. NMPA filings: which 欧玛 olive products are filed, filing numbers and dates, Chinese names, ingredient lists, 境内责任人, efficacy claims, 功效宣称依据摘要

### Takeaway
I found no NMPA filing record for any Phyto-C/欧玛 product: no 国妆网备进字 number, filing or change date, filed Chinese name, filed ingredient list, 境内责任人, efficacy category or 功效宣称依据摘要. The NMPA platform and its mirrors were unreachable, and search returned no snippet containing a filing. Indirect evidence suggests a general-trade (non-cross-border) channel exists alongside the cross-border stores: a domestic "欧玛官方京东自营旗舰店", and Tmall listings labelled 天猫精选 and 天猫超市. Products sold through such channels would normally need a 进口普通化妆品备案, so a filing probably exists but could not be seen.

### Inferences
- The domestic JD self-operated flagship store and the Tmall 天猫精选/天猫超市 listings point to general-trade imports. These need 进口普通化妆品备案, and since 2022 any efficacy claim also needs a published 功效宣称依据摘要 (the rule itself was not checked in this session; see Gaps). A filing very likely exists, plausibly under a name close to 「橄榄修复精华凝胶」 or 「橄榄修护精华」. This is an inference, not a finding.
- If the domestic operator is 上海梵朵斯国际贸易有限公司 (see §4), the filing number would read 国妆网备进字（沪）…; if it is 深圳市品妮国际贸易有限公司, 国妆网备进字（粤）…. This is inference only; neither company was confirmed as 境内责任人.
- The ¥410 JD third-party listing carries a note that overseas packaging changes often (§6). Together with the cross-border 包税 stores, this means several SKUs (cross-border US-label units and general-trade Chinese-label units) are probably on sale at once.

## 2. China IECIC 2021 (已使用化妆品原料目录 2021年版): entry numbers and 最高历史使用量 for the key raw materials; China's regulatory status for α-熊果苷

### Takeaway
A third-party database (李锦聪安评, hzpwjc.cn) gives 油橄榄（OLEA EUROPAEA）叶提取物 as IECIC 2021 No. **08138**, with a leave-on (驻留类) maximum historical use level of **32%** and no rinse-off value shown in the snippet. α-熊果苷 is No. **01005**, with no maximum level found. Chinese regulatory-consultancy articles say China sets no specific concentration limit for α-arbutin, unlike the EU (2% face cream / 0.5% body lotion since Regulation (EU) 2024/996). Values for 泛酸钙, 甘氨酸, 葡糖酸锌, 谷氨酰胺, 牛磺酸, 精氨酸 and 透明质酸钠 were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- **[3P-DB]** 油橄榄（OLEA EUROPAEA）叶提取物: IECIC 2021 序号 08138, CAS 8060-29-5, function given as skin protectant. The database states the IECIC 2021 驻留类 maximum historical use is **32%**. Its CIR citation reads 「浓度不高于（淋洗类为：0.3 %）（驻留类为：2 %）时，其用于化妆品是安全的」. Possible risk substance: pesticide residues. — [李锦聪安评 08138 完整版安全评估结论](http://www.hzpwjc.cn/gjpgjg/inci2021/08138.html); [hzpwjc CIR 08138](http://www.hzpwjc.cn/cir/inci2021/08138.html)
- **[3P-DB]** A second database repeats the CIR rinse-off 0.3% / leave-on 2% values for the same raw material; its URL code 01808138010 contains 08138. — [绿翊合规 lv-1.cn](https://www.lv-1.cn/yuanliao/inci/01808138010.html)
- **[3P-DB]** hzpwjc.cn notes that 「《已上市产品原料使用信息》将替代《已使用化妆品原料目录（2021版）》最高历史使用量」, so later NMPA data may supersede the 2021 values. — [hzpwjc CIR 08138](http://www.hzpwjc.cn/cir/inci2021/08138.html)
- **[MEDIA/3P]** Background: IECIC 2021 added maximum historical use levels for the first time. They are based on approved product applications in the 化妆品行政许可 system, and a raw material with only leave-on data may be used in rinse-off products by reference to it (snippet summary). — [界面新闻 首增最高历史用量](https://www.jiemian.com/article/5605454.html); [新浪科技 mirror, 2021-01-27](https://finance.sina.com.cn/tech/2021-01-27/doc-ikftpnny2246830.shtml); [中贸合规中心](https://www.zmuni.com/zh-hans/news/guan-yu-yi-shi-yong-hua-zhuang-pin-yuan-liao-mu-lu-de-zhei/)
- **[3P-DB]** α-熊果苷: 「该原料已收录于《已使用化妆品原料目录(2021年版)》序号为01005」. CIR: safe at 驻留类 ≤2%. No IECIC maximum historical use value appeared in the snippets. — [hzpwjc α-熊果苷 01005](http://www.hzpwjc.cn/cir/inci2021/01005.html); [hzpwjc 安评 01005](http://www.hzpwjc.cn/apgj/inci2021/01005.html)
- **[MEDIA/consultancy]** 「曲酸、α-熊果苷和熊果苷,虽然也被收录于《已使用化妆品原料目录(2021年版)》中,但并未设定具体的浓度限制」; and 「2021版《已使用化妆品原料目录》中大部分原料无历史使用量数据参考」. — [瑞旭集团 如何合理应用常见祛斑美白功能原料](https://www.cirs-group.com/cn/cosmetics/ru-he-he-li-ying-yong-chang-jian-qu-ban-mei-bai-gong-neng-yuan-liao-fu-guo-nei-wai-xian-liang-yao-qiu)
- **[OFFICIAL-reported]** International comparators (snippet summaries):
  - EU SCCS final opinion, early 2023: α-arbutin is safe up to 2% in face cream and 0.5% in body lotion, with hydroquinone kept as low as possible. — [瑞旭 SCCS 最终意见](https://www.cirs-group.com/cn/cosmetics/ou-meng-sccs-fa-bu-guan-yu-xiong-guo-gan-he-xiong-guo-gan-de-zui-zhong-yi-jian)

Non-compliant products may not be placed on the market from 2025-02-01 or made available from 2025-11-01. — [中国医药报 熊果苷等被欧盟限用](https://bk.cnpharm.com/zgyyb/2025/01/09/app_319504.html); [新浪 一批明星成分被限用了](https://finance.sina.cn/2024-04-16/detail-inarywyu5960978.d.html)
  - Australia: α-arbutin below 2% in face products and below 0.5% in body products, hydroquinone below 10 mg/kg. Taiwan: arbutin up to 7%, hydroquinone below 20 ppm. — [瑞旭 祛斑美白原料限量](https://www.cirs-group.com/cn/cosmetics/ru-he-he-li-ying-yong-chang-jian-qu-ban-mei-bai-gong-neng-yuan-liao-fu-guo-nei-wai-xian-liang-yao-qiu)
- **[OFFICIAL-reported]** 2023-08-23: NMPA published a revision of 《化妆品安全技术规范》 that included a test method beginning 「化妆品中α-熊…」 (the snippet was cut off, so it is unclear whether a limit was involved). 2023-02-17: 中检院 invited suggestions for 2023 revisions of the 规范, deadline 2023-03-17. — [瑞旭 全球化妆品法规动态第11期](https://www.cirs-group.com/cn/cosmetics/quan-qiu-hua-zhuang-pin-fa-gui-dong-tai-di-11-qi-2023-nian-1-yue-2-yue); [新疆药监局 转载](https://mpa.xinjiang.gov.cn/xjyjj/yzaq/202304/07d011568dc04ad58be1123e91248cd6.shtml)

### Inferences
- The 10% olive-leaf-extract claim (§5) is well below the 32% IECIC leave-on historical maximum reported by hzpwjc. At 10% it is above the CIR 2% leave-on safe-use figure, but CIR figures are not a Chinese legal limit. The real basis would depend on the extract's form (dry extract vs. extract in solvent), which is unknown.
- Since China sets no α-arbutin cap (per CIRS), the Chinese regulatory issue for α-熊果苷 in a 普通化妆品 is about claims (whitening), not concentration. The claims rule was not verified this session (see §1 Gaps).

### Gaps
- No IECIC 2021 entry numbers or 驻留类/淋洗类 maximums were retrieved for 泛酸钙, 甘氨酸, 葡糖酸锌, 谷氨酰胺, 牛磺酸, 精氨酸 or 透明质酸钠; the search budget ran out.
- No α-熊果苷 maximum historical use value was found, and the official IECIC 2021 Excel was not accessible.
- Not found: Chinese-regulator discussion in 2019–2021 of a 2% α-arbutin limit or hydroquinone-impurity limits. Only the EU, Australia and Taiwan figures and the truncated 2023 NMPA test-method item surfaced.
- The 32% value is from a third-party database and has not been checked against NMPA's official IECIC 2021 file.

## 3. Chinese ingredient lists vs the current US INCI for O-Live Gel: differences, and evidence of multiple Chinese versions over time

### Takeaway
No official (NMPA-filed) Chinese ingredient list was found. Chinese-language marketing and review sources give at least three differing lists. The one most often cited (Zhihu "丁妈深点评", Tmall copy) includes **积雪草 (Centella asiatica)** and "维生素B5", both absent from the current US INCI (Water, Olea Europaea Leaf Extract, **Calcium Pantothenate**, Glycine, Zinc Gluconate, **Alpha-Arbutin**, Glutamine, Taurine, Arginine, Sodium Hyaluronate, **Phenoxyethanol**). An ingredient-database "package list" gives **泛醇** where the US list has calcium pantothenate, and **熊果苷** where it has α-arbutin. No Chinese-sourced list seen names phenoxyethanol. So there was either an earlier formula with Centella and panthenol, or Chinese copy has been imprecise for years; without the NMPA record I cannot tell which.

## 6. Controversies: administrative penalties, 黑猫投诉, counterfeits, KOL disputes, "成分表与美版不一致", NMPA notices

### Takeaway
No administrative penalty, NMPA notice or KOL dispute naming 欧玛, 上海梵朵斯 or 深圳品妮 was found. The documented issues are: **counterfeits** (a 黑猫投诉 case about a 拼多多 purchase; a Zhihu account of a 咸鱼 fake confirmed by brand customer service); **polarized consumer reactions** (Douban "为啥你用就烂脸"); and frequent packaging changes noted by a JD cross-border seller. The big 2024 "油橄榄精华" scandal involved **CSS 希妍萃**, a product marketed as a cheap 欧玛 alternative, not 欧玛 itself. Its operator 上海景跃贸易有限公司 was fined ¥500,000 for false advertising after tests found no oleuropein.

### Inferences
- The counterfeit problem follows from high Tmall and JD price points (¥868–1,398 for 30 ml) and large price gaps between channels (third-party ¥410 vs. ¥1,104 at 京东国际会员店), with many authorization layers (cross-border vs. general trade, three claimed agents).
- The CSS case shows the main regulatory risk for any China "油橄榄" product: marker-compound testing (oleuropein, hydroxytyrosol) and label-vs-filing consistency. The ingredient-list discrepancies in §3 (Centella/panthenol/arbutin vs. the US INCI) are the kind of issue regulators examined in the CSS matter. No such action against 欧玛 was found.
