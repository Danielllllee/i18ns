# Olive leaf extract specifications, skin delivery and shelf stability of oleuropein (preliminary draft, to be superseded)

## Cosmetic-grade olive leaf ingredients: supplier specifications

### Takeaway
Cosmetic olive leaf ingredients are sold both as dry standardized powders (12%, 35%, 80% oleuropein) and as dilute liquids (about 0.4% to 7.5% oleuropein, or unstandardized), so "10% olive leaf extract" cannot be converted to an oleuropein percentage without knowing which one is used.

### Cited Findings
- Deretil Nature NaturOlive OE12S: INCI Olea Europaea Leaf Extract, solid, standardized to 12% oleuropein, made only with water — [SpecialChem OE12S](https://www.specialchem.com/cosmetics/product/deretil-nature-naturolive-oe12s)
- Deretil Nature NaturOlive OE35S: same INCI, solid, standardized to 35% oleuropein — [SpecialChem OE35S](https://www.specialchem.com/cosmetics/product/deretil-nature-naturolive-oe35s)
- Sabinsa Cosmetics "Oleuropein 80": INCI Olea Europaea (Olive) Leaf Extract, 80% oleuropein — [Knowde](https://www.knowde.com/stores/sabinsa-cosmetics/products/sabinsa-cosmetics-oleuropein-80)
- Berkem Olive Extract R0570: aqueous extract 10%, water 40%, plant-origin glycerin 50%; actives named oleuropein, hydroxytyrosol, tyrosol (no percentages found) — [Knowde](https://www.knowde.com/stores/berkem/products/berkem-olive-extract-r0570)
- Voyageur Soap & Candle "Olive Leaf Liquid Extract": olive leaf extract in propanediol standardized to 7.5% oleuropein (retailer page) — [Voyageur](https://www.voyageursoapandcandle.com/products/olive-leaf-liquid-extract)
- Lotioncrafter "Olive Leaf Extract": INCI Propanediol (and) Water (and) Olea Europaea (Olive) Leaf Extract, typical usage 2-5%, pH 4.5-5.5 as supplied, no oleuropein percentage stated — [Lotioncrafter](https://lotioncrafter.com/products/olive-leaf-extract)
- MySkinRecipes (Thai retailer) "Olive Extract (Hydroxytyrosol, Oleuropein)": specification minimum hydroxytyrosol 0.4% and minimum oleuropein 0.4% — [MySkinRecipes](https://myskinrecipes.com/shop/en/anti-irritant/1059-olive-extract-hydroxytyrosol-oleuropein.html)
