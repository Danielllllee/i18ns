# Phyto-C / PhytoCeuticals（欧玛）olive products in China, round 2: NMPA filings, dated Chinese ingredient lists, origins of Chinese marketing claims, trademarks, and the CSS 希妍萃 incident

Method and limits (please read first). Research ran on 2026-10-24 and builds on `china_market_regulatory.md` (round 1) in this folder. Under the network policy no page could be opened, so **every finding comes from WebSearch result snippets or the tool's summaries of them, not from full pages**. I ran 43 queries (41 standard, 2 extended). The search tool is US-based. It barely indexes NMPA's database, 美丽修行, 透明标签, 心上, Tmall/JD item pages or CNIPA: a `site:bevol.cn` query returned no bevol.cn pages at all. Text in 「」 is reproduced exactly as the tool returned it. Some of it is the tool's summary wording rather than verified page text, and is marked "(as summarized)". The tool also mis-renders some names; for example, it wrote 「罗旺宇」 for the KOL 骆王宇 (pinyin Luo Wangyu). Unless a publication date is given, the date was not retrievable; the access date for everything is 2026-10-24. Evidence tiers: **[OFFICIAL]** government or registry record · **[3P]** third-party database or event-organizer record · **[BRAND]** brand, China operator or authorized-store copy · **[AGENCY]** marketing deck of unconfirmed authorship · **[MEDIA]** industry media, self-media or shopping guides · **[CONSUMER]** consumer posts. "Not found" means not visible through this search tool. It does not mean the record does not exist.

### Inferences
- The entry-channel trail is cross-border or professional at every step found: an HK-registered Asia entity at a Shanghai aesthetic-medicine congress (2015), a 网易考拉 brand page, and the 天猫国际 "PhytoCeuticals海外旗舰店" from 2020 (round 1). This fits years of selling without a general-trade NMPA filing. It would also explain why the Chinese ingredient lists in circulation (§2) are translations of US lists rather than filed lists. The exemption of cross-border retail imports from filing was **not verified this session**.
- If a general-trade filing exists, it most likely dates from the period when domestic channels appeared: 上海梵朵斯 was established 2022-10-26, and JD 自营 and 天猫超市 listings appear around 2023–2024 (round 1). That places it under the post-2021 regime, so its number would read 「国妆网备进字（沪）…」 if 上海梵朵斯 is the 境内责任人, or 「国妆网备进字（粤）…」 if 深圳品妮 is (inference carried from round 1).
- **A concrete test once the filing is found:** no Chinese-language list found so far contains 泛酸钙 or 苯氧乙醇 (§2). A filing for the current (V2) gel would have to list 苯氧乙醇 (phenoxyethanol, the preservative) and would most likely list 泛酸钙 (calcium pantothenate). If it did, that would show the Chinese marketing lists are out of date. If the filed list instead shows 积雪草 and no preservative, a China-specific or older formula was filed.

## 2. Chinese ingredient lists for the gel and cream (date and source), compared with the current US INCI and older US versions

### Takeaway
**No dated Chinese ingredient list was found.** The undated Chinese gel lists from both rounds form one family. Each is a translation of the **older US "V1" list**, which has "Vitamin B5", "Arbutin", L-amino acids and no preservative. Most add **积雪草**, which mirrors a centella variant seen on US professional-retailer pages. The 丁妈 Zhihu English list matches the US V1 order item for item, except that Water is dropped and Centella is inserted second. **No Chinese list matching the current US INCI (V2) was found.** Searches pairing 欧玛 with 泛酸钙 or 苯氧乙醇 returned nothing. 可爱网 explicitly states 「防腐剂成分：0种」. For the cream, the only Chinese-language list remains a Hong Kong retailer's key-ingredient line (round 1).

### Inferences
- **The Chinese "积雪草" list is a translated US list, not a China formula.** The 丁妈 English list keeps the same order of nine items as the US V1 list (Olive Leaf Extract → Vitamin B5 → Glycine → Zinc Gluconate → Arbutin → L-Glutamine → Taurine → L-Arginine → Sodium Hyaluronate). It differs only in dropping Water, inserting "Centella Asiatica" second, and spelling "Arbutine". That matches the centella variant on US professional-retailer pages. The Chinese sources therefore add no independent evidence that a centella-containing gel was ever made. That question (`formulations_international.md` §3) stays open.
- **「泛醇」 is probably a mistranslation of the ambiguous V1 "Vitamin B5", not evidence of panthenol.** V2 spells the B5 form out as calcium pantothenate (moderate confidence).
- **「熊果苷」 carries V1's ambiguity ("Arbutin"/"Arbutin Glycoside").** The 2023–24 Tmall copy's 「α-熊果苷」 matches V2 naming but still lists 积雪草 and no preservative. It is therefore a hybrid marketing list, not a label transcription.
- **The cream is unclear.** The HK listing calls a **50 g** jar 「升級」 (upgraded) and gives V2-type actives (維他命A, B5, E, 積雪草). Sibling notes say V1 was a 50 g jar and V2 a 60 g pump. Either an intermediate "upgraded" 50 g version existed, or the retailer mixed the two descriptions (low confidence).
