**Method, limits and conventions (read first).** Research date: 2026-10-25.

What these notes build on:
- They extend `china_products.md` (same folder) and do not repeat it. Facts already there are marked "(earlier notes)" and are restated only where the product table needs them.
- Sibling notes in this folder already cover Vytrus OLEA VITAE, Hallstar Eurol BT's published use levels, 浙江熙正霖's press release and the PureXerum oleocanthal serum (`ingredient_suppliers_claims.md`, `global_products.md`). Those facts are not repeated here.

How the search went:
- **Searches:** about 53 WebSearch calls in all.
  - Without filters, Chinese brand-name queries returned only generic olive-oil pages (DHC, PURITO, Sigma-Aldrich, a Plants-vs-Zombies wiki).
  - Restricting results to Chinese domains (smzdm, zhihu, sina, sohu, 163, jd, toutiao, weibo, baidu) in "extended" mode worked, and almost all findings below come from those runs.
  - Two queries were rejected because the domain list included hosts the search tool cannot access (taobao.com, pinduoduo.com). Both were re-run without them.
- **Fetches:** all WebFetch attempts failed with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`: www.hallstarbeauty.com (the "Olive Miracles" PDF), glenncorp.com (the Eurol BT PIS PDF) and m.cosdna.com (an ingredient list). None was retried. No page body was read directly.

How to read the quotes:
- Text in 「」 marked **(page title)** is the verbatim title or snippet from the search index. This is the most reliable verbatim Chinese available here.
- Text in 「」 marked **(quoted by tool)** was presented by the search tool as page text, but the page itself was not seen.
- **(as summarized)** marks the search tool's own condensation.
- **(not disambiguated)** means the tool did not say which result in a set a statement came from. The whole set is listed.
- Dates come from the URL when the page date was not retrieved, and are marked "(date from URL)".

Evidence tiers, as in the earlier notes:
- **[OFFICIAL]** regulator or patent record
- **[3P-DB]** third-party regulatory or ingredient database, or compliance consultancy
- **[BRAND]** brand, operator, PR or store copy
- **[SUPPLIER]** raw-material vendor or distributor
- **[MEDIA]** news, industry or self-media
- **[KOL]** influencer statements
- **[CONSUMER]** consumer posts

## 1. What olive products do the 12 domestic brands from the 14-brand roundup sell (百植萃, 诠润, 瑞沛, 森芝玑, 艾酸氏, purid, 春日来信, 成分星球, 言之有物, 至本, 米蓓尔, 毕生之研), and what do they claim and disclose?

### Takeaway
All 12 brands were matched to a specific olive product. One of them, 至本, sells a toner/emulsion pair rather than a serum. That pair is the "unnamed 5% Oleaxcel™ product" in the earlier notes.

What the claims and disclosures show:
- **The percentages measure different things, and none measures oleuropein or hydroxytyrosol.** Every percentage found is for a trade material or a liquid "提取液/精粹/溶液", or is a share of a proprietary complex:
  - 诠润: 3% 油橄榄叶提取液
  - 瑞沛: 5% 油橄榄叶精粹
  - 米蓓尔: 3% 油橄榄叶提取物
  - 春日来信: 2% 油橄榄
  - 至本: 5% Oleaxcel™
  - 神秘博士: 30% 油橄榄溶液 (three ingredients combined)
- **Supplier:** only one named supplier surfaced. A brand-linked article says 森芝玑 uses **Hallstar EUROL® BT**, the same family of material as John Jeff's.
- **Not found for any of the 12:** the olive ingredient's position in the NMPA-filed ingredient list, a 功效宣称依据摘要, a human test, or a third-party marker test.
- **Irritant signals for redness-prone skin** come from reviews and promotional posts, not from verified ingredient lists:
  - 神秘博士: contains 留兰香 (spearmint) extract, and a promotional post calls the brand 「巨香」.
  - 米蓓尔 小蓝帽: 「植物调香」.
  - 至本 净肌平衡 water/emulsion: contains 白柳树皮提取物, a natural salicylate source.
  - Reviewers describe 百植萃 and 成分星球 as free of alcohol and fragrance. This is unverified.
- **Price:** most of these sell for roughly ¥40–170 per 15–30 ml, an order of magnitude below 欧玛 (earlier notes).

### Inferences
- **None of the 12 brands discloses on a comparable basis.** Some labels name a liquid ("提取液", "溶液"), others an unspecified "精粹/提取物", a trade material (Oleaxcel™) or a proprietary complex (毕立舒, 30% 三重). None states marker content. John Jeff (§2) remains the only brand found disclosing a dry-extract basis.
- **The roundup's "concentration" label is not borne out.** Of its five 「浓度党」 brands, only John Jeff (dry %) and 瑞沛 (5% 精粹) give a number. 安修泽's "40%" is a complex (§2), and 森芝玑 and 艾酸氏 gave no olive figure (艾酸氏's 10% and 5% are B5).
- **Hallstar Eurol BT appears to be a shared base material.** It has now been linked to two domestic brands, John Jeff and 森芝玑. At Hallstar's published use levels (0.1–1%, earlier notes), a brand quoting a large "%" is most likely quoting the solution.
- **"三重油橄榄" may not mean three *Olea* ingredients.** In Chinese standard names, 「橄榄」 alone usually denotes *Canarium album* (Chinese white olive), while 油橄榄 is *Olea europaea*. 神秘博士's 「橄榄叶提取物＋油橄榄叶提取物」 pairing may therefore include a non-*Olea* ingredient. The filed INCI list was not seen, and the IECIC entry for a Canarium leaf extract was not checked. Treat this as a hypothesis to verify.
- **Irritant guidance for redness-prone buyers, on current evidence:**
  - Approach with caution: 神秘博士 (spearmint extract plus a "巨香" brand reputation) and 米蓓尔 (「植物调香」).
  - 至本's water/emulsion contains willow bark, a natural salicylate source, which matters to anyone reacting to 水杨酸.
  - 百植萃 and 成分星球 are only *claimed* by reviewers to be alcohol- and fragrance-free.
  - None of this replaces reading the filed list, which none of these sources showed.

### Inferences
- **John Jeff's two dry-extract figures fit one trade solution, and the old "5%" does not.** The arithmetic is mine:
  - 0.5775 ÷ 7.7 = 0.075, and 0.75 ÷ 10 = 0.075. Both of the brand's figures imply a trade solution with **7.5% dry extract**.
  - On that basis, the original "5%" label would hold only 0.375% dry. Yet the brand said the 5% version also held 0.5775% dry, which would need an 11.55%-solids solution.
  - So the "5%" was either on another material, on another basis, or wrong. That would explain the 「反向升级」 dispute (earlier notes).
  - The third generation's 1.325% dry would need about 17.7% of a 7.5% solution.
  - The Zhihu "约10%" figure for Eurol BT's olive content is in the same range as 7.5%. Both are unverified.
- **The 1.325% dry dose is far above the supplier's levels.** Hallstar's published levels (0.1–1% for skin care, earlier notes; 0.1–0.3% for toiletries on the distributor sheet) refer to the trade material as supplied, not to dry extract.
  - At the implied 7.5% solids, 1.325% dry extract means about 17.7% trade material. That is roughly 18–177× Hallstar's 0.1–1% skin-care range.
  - So if the 1.325% is literal dry extract, John Jeff doses far above the supplier's guidance.
  - If "Gly" is a different grade with other solids, the numbers cannot be checked without its spec.
- **安修泽's numbers are relative or blended.** Its "40%" is a blend and its "152%" (earlier notes) a relative figure. Its only absolute marker numbers come from self-media whose units contradict each other.
- **The 28-day test is minimally described.** What is disclosed (between-group, 28 days, 叶理 / 南方医科大学皮肤病医院化妆品检测中心) is consistent with a standard cosmetic efficacy evaluation.
